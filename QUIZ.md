## Part 1 — Bucket Drip (Torricelli's Law)

**1.** Torricelli's Law states that the exit speed of water from a hole at the bottom of a bucket is v = √(2gh), where h is the water height. If the water level drops from 40 cm to 10 cm, by what factor does the exit speed change?

- a) It stays the same
- b) It doubles
- c) **It halves**
- d) It drops to one quarter

---

- a) Speed is constant regardless of height
- b) Speed is proportional to *h*
- c) **Speed is proportional to √h**
- d) Speed is proportional to *h²*
